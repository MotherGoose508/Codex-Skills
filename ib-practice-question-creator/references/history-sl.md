# History SL

These checks supplement, but never replace, the current official guide and specimen materials for the learner's 2027 examination session.

- Read `../../ib-history-revision/SKILL.md` when available.
- Clarify the applicable paper, prescribed subject, world-history topic, region, period, or source task rather than inferring it from a broad classroom topic.
- Do not invent historical quotations, statistics, provenance, or historians' views. Clearly label constructed sources and do not use them as evidence of what a historical actor actually said.
- Build essay prompts around genuine contestable judgments and source questions around the verified component structure.
- Ensure the internal marking logic rewards accurate, relevant evidence and argument rather than a single predetermined interpretation.
