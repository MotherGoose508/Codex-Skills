# Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL

These checks supplement, but never replace, the current official guide and specimen materials for the learner's 2027 examination session.

- Confirm the paper or permitted technology when it affects the task; do not assume calculator access.
- Match the verified balance of algebraic reasoning, proof, modelling, interpretation, and multi-step problem solving.
- Validate every expression, domain restriction, diagram, numerical value, and claimed solution. Check that rounded answers are consistent with the given data.
- Do not create artificial difficulty through gratuitous arithmetic or ambiguous notation.
- No sibling mathematics revision skill currently exists, so keep all reasoning and marking self-contained and grounded in authoritative course material.
